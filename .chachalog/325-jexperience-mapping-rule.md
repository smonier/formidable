Added automatic jExperience mapping rules: a published form with mapped fields feeds the visitor profile (#325)

Map a field to a visitor profile property in the form editor and publish the form: jExperience gets the rule that writes each submission into the profile, updated at every publication and removed when the form is unpublished or deleted. Nothing to configure in jExperience.
